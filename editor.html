Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music video/playlist link, an HTTPS audio URL, or a local MP3/OGG/WAV path. YouTube channel and artist pages cannot play directly. YouTube playback uses its embedded player; its volume is controlled inside the player, not by the volume field. Autoplay with sound is not requested; visitors press Play.
